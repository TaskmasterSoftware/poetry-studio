---
name: Poetry form check confidence
description: Keep automated poetry-form checks advisory and transparent about uncertainty.
---

Treat detected differences as potential diversions, not errors. Syllable counts can fall back to spelling-based estimates, and rhyme checks cannot settle slant rhyme, dialect, meter, or intentional variation. Each report is a snapshot and should be refreshed after editing.

**Why:** Poetic forms are interpretive, and dictionary-based checks cannot reliably judge every pronunciation or valid variant.

**How to apply:** Preserve explicit estimates and per-form limitations when adding checks or changing the results UI; avoid presenting a heuristic match as proof that a poem is correct.
